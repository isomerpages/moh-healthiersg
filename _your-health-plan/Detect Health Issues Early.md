<h1><strong>Why early detection matters</strong></h1>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>Chronic conditions like diabetes, high cholesterol and high blood pressure
often develop silently without obvious symptoms, making regular health
screening essential</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Early detection through regular screening allows for more effective treatment,
better outcomes and good management of health conditions</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Stay protected against preventable diseases through recommended vaccinations</p>
</li>
</ul>
<hr>
<h2><strong>Support throughout&nbsp;your health journey</strong></h2>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>Build a lasting relationship with your Healthier SG doctor who understands
your health history and lifestyle habits, enabling more personalised and
effective care</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Regular check-ins with the same doctor ensure consistent monitoring of
your health status and early detection of any changes</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Your Healthier SG doctor provides continuous guidance and support, helping
you better understand and manage your health conditions through every stage
of life</p>
</li>
</ul>
<hr>
<h2><strong>Your personalised Health Plan</strong></h2>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>Receive recommended Healthier SG screening tests and vaccinations based
on your health needs and age group</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Get guidance from your Healthier SG doctor who will help you understand
your screening results and provide appropriate follow-up care</p>
</li>
</ul>
