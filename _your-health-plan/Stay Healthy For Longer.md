<h2><strong>What is a Health Plan?</strong></h2>
<p>It is a simple guide that captures your Health Plan consultation with
your doctor. It includes steps you can take to stay healthy or manage your
chronic condition(s), and Healthier SG screening tests and vaccinations
that are recommended for you. After your first Health Plan consultation,
your doctor will review your Health Plan at annual check-ins.</p>
<p>You can easily view your Health Plan on <a href="https://for.sg/healthhubhsg" rel="noopener nofollow" target="_blank">HealthHub</a>. You will be able to see:</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>Details of your discussion with your Healthier SG doctor</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>The health goals that you and your doctor agreed would be suitable for
you</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Diet and exercise recommendations with supporting resources</p>
</li>
</ul>
<p>Your Health Plan gets updated as you progress in your Healthier SG journey,
even if you enrol in a different Healthier SG clinic.</p>
<hr>
<h2><strong>Your Health Plan consultations and annual check-ins are fully subsidised</strong></h2>
<p>Your first Health Plan consultation and subsequent annual check-ins with
your Healthier SG doctor are fully subsidised. You will also receive special
subsidies for Healthier SG screening tests and vaccinations at your Healthier
SG clinic (for Singapore Citizens only).</p>
<p>However, if you consult your doctor for treatment (e.g., for flu, abdominal
pain), and agree to receive these services, the clinic’s prevailing charges
will apply. Investigations performed that are not part of Healthier SG
screening tests and vaccinations will be charged at the clinic's prevailing
rates (e.g., blood tests to monitor chronic condition(s)).</p>
<hr>
<h2><strong>Creating your Health Plan at your first Health Plan consultation</strong></h2>
<p>At your first Health Plan consultation, your doctor will create your Health
Plan after getting to know you and your health, and explaining what you
can do to stay healthy or manage your chronic condition(s).</p>

<h2><strong>Why should I go for annual Health Plan check-ins?</strong></h2>
<p>Regular check-ins allow your Healthier SG doctor to understand your overall
health and progress toward your health goals. Your doctor will refer to
and update your Health Plan based on your changing health needs.</p>
<p>If you have chronic conditions, these check-ins with your Healthier SG
doctor will play a crucial role in managing your health and setting targets
for optimal control. This could include weight management, smoking cessation
and other specific interventions required to manage chronic conditions.</p>
<h4><strong>Frequency of Health Plan check-ins</strong></h4>
<p>How often you need to check in with your Healthier SG doctor depends on
whether you have any chronic condition.</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>If you are generally healthy with no chronic conditions, you are recommended
to check in with your Healthier SG doctor once a year. The check-in may
be done in-person or over the phone (your Healthier SG clinic will advise
on the mode of check-in). An in-person check-in must be done once every
three years.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>If you have one or more chronic conditions, your doctor will recommend
two or more check-in visits in the year to better review and manage your
condition(s). An in-person check-in must be done at least once a year.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>If you have transferred your enrolment to a new Healthier SG doctor, you&nbsp;are
recommended&nbsp;to make an in-person visit for the first check-in so that
the doctor can better understand your health status and address any outstanding
health concerns.&nbsp;</p>
</li>
</ul>
<p>You may make a check-in appointment with your clinic, or your clinic will
contact you to schedule your check-in.</p>
<hr>
<h2><strong>What to do after your Health Plan consultation or check-ins</strong></h2>
<p>After discussing your Health Plan with your Healthier SG doctor, continue
your Healthier SG journey by:</p>
<p>○ Viewing your Health Plan on HealthHub</p>
<p>○ Taking steps toward your health goals in your Health Plan, such as:</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>Using the <a href="https://go.gov.sg/healthy365" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" target="_blank"><u>Healthy 365</u></a>&nbsp;app
to track progress toward your activity goals, access healthy lifestyle
activities, receive recommended resources and programmes, and earn rewards
for completing challenges</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Visiting your nearest Active Ageing Centre for programmes and services,&nbsp;such
as physical and social activities and vital signs monitoring</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Getting your family and friends to join you as you work toward your health
goals, such as exercising or trying healthier recipes together</p>
</li>
</ul>
<p>Remember to share what you have accomplished with your Healthier SG doctor
at your next Health Plan check-in!</p>
