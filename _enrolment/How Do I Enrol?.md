[Healthier SG enrolment programme will begin on 5 July 2023] 

Under Healthier SG, you can enrol with a Healthier SG clinic and your family clinic will develop a personalised Health Plan with you.

###   Am I eligible and when can I enrol?

From July 2023, the Healthier SG enrolment programme will begin with Singapore Citizens and Permanent Residents aged 60 years and above. They will be progressively invited via SMS to enrol with either a Healthier SG clinic or polyclinic. 

Those aged 40 to 59 years will be invited on a later date.

Eligible residents will progressively receive SMS invites to enrol via HealthHub.

###   How do I enrol when I am eligible? Where can I enrol?
![steps](/images/Enrolment_Benefits/enrolment%20mobile.png)

**1. Self-enrol**

You can self-enrol using HealthHub. 

**2. Enrol with caregiver's assistance**

You can grant access to your caregiver/family member/loved one by adding him/her in HealthHub, so he/she can enrol you on your behalf using his/her smartphone. You can follow the step-by-step guide in [this page](/enrolment/caregivers/). 

**3. Assisted enrolment**

Approach the Healthier SG Ambassadors at Community Centres (CC) or Joint Testing and Vaccination Centres (JTVC) for assistance. Please bring your letter and your NRIC/FIN. To check for an enrolment station near you, visit this [page](https://www.gowhere.gov.sg/healthiersg).

### Do you need help enrolling to Healthier SG?

You can grant access to your family members or loved ones to assist you with your enrolment by adding them as your caregiver in HealthHub and enabling permission to manage Healthier SG on your behalf. Your caregiver can then add you as a care recipient to view your health records and manage your e-Services on HealthHub.

Follow the steps below to first add a caregiver.

![faq](/images/FAQs/caregiver1.png)
![faq](/images/FAQs/caregiver2.png)

Please ensure that you have enabled “Healthier SG” access in the Caregiver’s Details page (as shown in Step 5). 

After this has been done, inform your caregiver to add your Health Profile under **Add a Care Recipient**.

![faq](/images/FAQs/recipient1.png)
![faq](/images/FAQs/recipient2.png)

Once your caregiver has successfully added you as a care recipient, your user profile will be shown on HealthHub for them to assist with your enrolment if you are eligible to enrol in Healthier SG.

Alternatively, you may enrol in Healthier SG through the HealthHub web portal at https://www.healthhub.sg by logging in with your Singpass if you do not wish to download the app or do not have access to a smartphone.
