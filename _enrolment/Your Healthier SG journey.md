<h3>Step 1: Register</h3>
<div class="isomer-image-wrapper">
<img style="width: 100%" height="auto" width="100%" alt="enrol" src="/images/enrolment_benefits/image1.jpg">
</div>
<ul>
<li>
<p>Enrol when you receive MOH’s SMS invitation.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Download the HealthHub app.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Choose your preferred Healthier SG clinic.</p>
</li>
</ul>
<p>For a list of Healthier SG clinics, click <a href="https://gowhere.gov.sg/healthiersg" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" target="_blank">here</a>.</p>
<h3>Step 2: See your doctor</h3>
<div class="isomer-image-wrapper">
<img style="width: 100%" height="auto" width="100%" alt="enrol" src="/images/enrolment_benefits/image2.jpg">
</div>
<ul>
<li>
<p>Schedule your first Health Plan consultation, which will be fully subsidised.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Create your Health Plan with your family doctor.</p>
</li>
</ul>
<h3>Step 3: Participate</h3>
<div class="isomer-image-wrapper">
<img style="width: 100%" height="auto" width="100%" alt="enrol" src="/images/enrolment_benefits/image3.jpg">
</div>
<ul>
<li>
<p>Follow your personalised Health Plan.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Participate in activities on the Healthy 365 app.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Check in with your family doctor at least once a year.</p>
</li>
</ul>
