<h3>Healthier SG Chronic Tier</h3><p></p><p>To ensure affordability of common chronic medications across GP clinics and polyclinics, the Healthier SG Chronic Tier takes effect from <a rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" target="_blank">1 February 2024</a>. Under this subsidy framework, selected common chronic medications are offered to residents enrolled at Healthier SG GP clinics at subsidised rates comparable to polyclinics. The subsidies apply for treatment of chronic conditions only at the enrolled clinic.</p><h4>Who is eligible for the Healthier SG Chronic Tier?</h4><p>Those enrolled in Healthier SG with higher chronic medication needs and with bills which exceed the current CHAS annual subsidy limits are more likely to benefit from the Healthier SG Chronic Tier. Patients with lower medication needs may continue to use existing CHAS subsidies. You should consult with your family doctor to assess your suitability for the Healthier SG Chronic Tier subsidies.</p><h4>How can I tell if I am better off using the Healthier SG Chronic Tier?</h4><p>Not all Healthier SG enrollees may be better off using the Healthier SG Chronic Tier. Those with no chronic conditions, low medication needs or who do not exceed the annual limits for CHAS chronic subsidies may not need to use the Healthier SG Chronic Tier subsidies since they would be well covered under the existing CHAS chronic subsidies. You should consult your family doctor to assess your suitability for the Healthier SG Chronic Tier subsides. &nbsp;(see worked examples here.)</p><p>You can use only one subsidy framework at each visit, either the CHAS Chronic Tier or the Healthier SG Chronic Tier. You can alternate between the two subsidy frameworks to cater for any future changes in medication needs. The remaining balance of the annual dollar subsidy will be pro-rated at your next visit when you switch between subsidy frameworks. This is to account for the difference in annual limits between the two subsidy frameworks.</p><h4>Medication delivery</h4><p>If your enrolled clinic does not carry stock of medication prescribed to you from the Healthier SG list of selected chronic medications, the clinic can arrange for direct delivery to your home at a nominal delivery fee.&nbsp;</p><p>Click here to view the Healthier SG Medication List</p>
