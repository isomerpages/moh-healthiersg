<h3>Healthier SG Chronic Tier</h3><p>To ensure affordability of common chronic medications at GP clinics and polyclinics, the Healthier SG Chronic Tier takes effect from February 2024. Under this subsidy framework, <a href="/enrolment/healthier-sg-chronic-tier/medication-list/" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" target="_blank">selected common chronic medications</a> are offered to residents enrolled at Healthier SG GP clinics at subsidised rates comparable to polyclinics. The subsidies apply for treatment of chronic conditions only at the enrolled clinic.</p><h4>Who is eligible for the Healthier SG Chronic Tier?</h4><p>Under the Healthier SG subsidy framework, all Community Health Assist Scheme (CHAS), Pioneer Generation (PG) and Merdeka Generation (MG) cardholders can get Healthier SG Chronic Tier subsidies for medications on the <a href="/enrolment/healthier-sg-chronic-tier/medication-list/" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" target="_blank">Healthier SG Medication List</a> at their enrolled clinic.</p><h4>How can I tell if I am better off using the Healthier SG Chronic Tier?</h4><p>For most Singaporean patients, the cost of medications for chronic diseases at their enrolled GP clinic is already covered under CHAS. The Healthier SG Chronic Tier will benefit CHAS/PG/MG cardholders with higher medication needs and whose bills may exceed the current CHAS annual subsidy limits. Please consult your family doctor if the Healthier SG Chronic Tier is suitable for your medication needs (see worked example</p><p>below).</p><p>You can use only one subsidy framework at each visit, either the CHAS Chronic Tier or the Healthier SG Chronic Tier. You can alternate between the two subsidy frameworks to cater for any future changes in medication needs. The remaining balance in the annual dollar subsidy will be pro-rated at your next visit when you switch between subsidy frameworks. This is to account for the difference in annual limits between the two subsidy frameworks.</p><h4>Medication delivery</h4><p>Certain GP clinics may also arrange for direct delivery of selected chronic medications under the Healthier SG Medication List to the enrollee's home. Delivery charges may apply.</p><p><a href="/enrolment/healthier-sg-chronic-tier/medication-list/" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" target="_blank">Click here for the Healthier SG Medication
