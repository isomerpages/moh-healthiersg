Under the Healthier SG Chronic Tier, CHAS/PG/MG cardholders can get selected medications for common chronic conditions at their enrolled Healthier SG GP clinic, at prices that are comparable to those at polyclinics. 

The Healthier SG Medication List comprises of medications which are commonly used in polyclinics to manage conditions under the Chronic Disease Management Programme (CDMP). Enrollees can discuss suitable medications with their GPs but they cannot specify the brand of medication that their GP prescribes.
