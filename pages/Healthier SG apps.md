### HealthHub

Download the [HealthHub app](https://for.sg/hh-hsg) to:
* Enrol to Healthier SG and choose your preferred clinic
* Schedule your appointments
* Access your personalised Health Plan
* View records of your health screenings and immunisations
* Keep track of your health goals

![https://for.sg/hh-hsg](/images/download%20healthhub.jpeg)

### Healthy 365

Download the [Healthy 365 app](https://go.gov.sg/downloadhealthy365) to:
* Register for healthy lifestyle programmes and activities near you
* Track your physical activity and diet
* Earn Healthpoints by staying active
* Redeem Healthpoints ith a catalogue of lifestyle rewards

![https://go.gov.sg/downloadhealthy365](/images/download_healthy365.png)
