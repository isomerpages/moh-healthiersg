### HealthHub

![img](/images/health%20apps/healthhubapp.png)

The HealthHub app is the national population platform for digital health. 

**Through HealthHub, you can**:
* Enrol in Healthier SG; 
* Choose your preferred Healthier SG clinic;
* Schedule your medical appointments;
* Access your personalised Health Plan;
* View records of your health screenings and immunisations;
* Keep track of your health goals. 

**Download the HealthHub app [here](https://for.sg/hh-hsg).**

For more information on HealthHub, visit this [page](https://www.healthhub.sg/).
 
### Healthy 365: Discover programmes and offerings for a Healthier You. 

![img](/images/health%20apps/healthy365.png)

* View your health-related lifestyle data at a glance – track your daily physical activity and sleep duration with a compatible wearable and use the meal log tool to monitor your daily calorie intake.
* Take healthier actions by:
	- Getting started with challenges such as the National Steps Challenge™ and Eat, Drink, Shop Healthy Challenge.
	- Participating in programmes (e.g. Live Well, Age Well programme) that are recommended to you.
	-	Booking activities including a wide variety of free exercise classes and hands-on health workshops.
* Enjoy access to informative resources and partner programmes.
* Earn Healthpoints for taking healthier actions. 
* Redeem Healthpoints by choosing from a catalogue of lifestyle rewards

**Download the Healthy 365 app at [go.gov.sg/downloadhealthy365](https://go.gov.sg/downloadhealthy365) today!**
